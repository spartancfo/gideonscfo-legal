Privacy Policy

Gideons CFO Platform  |  Effective Date: May 13, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 13, 2026

Gideons CFO LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Gideons CFO Platform, an AI-powered financial analysis service provided to business clients. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our platform.

By accessing or using the Gideons CFO Platform, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and business name provided during onboarding.
  • Financial Data: Data retrieved from connected third-party services such as QuickBooks Online, ERP systems, and other financial platforms you authorize us to access. This may include chart of accounts, accounts receivable, accounts payable, transaction history, payroll data, and financial reports.
  • Usage Data: Questions and queries submitted to the AI CFO, session logs, and workflow execution records.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser type, and connection logs collected for security and operational purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Provide and operate the Gideons CFO Platform and AI financial analysis services.
  • Retrieve and process your authorized financial data to respond to your queries.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the platform.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or service updates.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your financial data to train AI models, sell advertising, or share data with unaffiliated third parties for their own commercial purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is processed and stored on dedicated, isolated infrastructure hosted on private servers. Each client's data is maintained in a separate, isolated environment. We implement the following security measures:

  • Full-disk encryption on all server storage (FileVault).
  • Encrypted transmission via TLS/HTTPS for all data in transit.
  • VPN-only access to client environments via WireGuard.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.
  • Automated daily backups with 7-day retention.

4. Third-Party Services

The Gideons CFO Platform integrates with third-party services you authorize, including:

  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit): Used to retrieve your financial data via OAuth 2.0. Subject to Intuit's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: Used to power AI analysis. Queries and financial data included in AI requests are transmitted to OpenAI's API. OpenAI's API terms state that data submitted via the API is not used to train OpenAI models by default. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Other ERP/Business Systems: Additional integrations you authorize (e.g., KatanaMRP, AscendTMS) are subject to their respective privacy policies.

We will inform you prior to enabling any new third-party integration that processes your financial data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information with third parties except:

  • As necessary to operate the platform (e.g., AI processing via OpenAI API as described above).
  • As required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • With your explicit written consent.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for the duration of your service agreement. Upon termination of services, we will delete or return your data within 30 days upon written request. Backup copies are retained for up to 7 days after deletion.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal and financial data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data upon termination of services.
  • Revoke authorization for third-party integrations at any time.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format upon request.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Gideons CFO Platform is intended for use by businesses and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or through the platform. Continued use of the platform after notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
